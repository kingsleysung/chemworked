The molarity formula

Molarity (M) is moles of solute per litre of solution:

M = n ÷ V   and   n = m ÷ (molar mass)

Put them together to go straight between grams and molarity: m = M × V × (molar mass)

Worked example

How many grams of NaCl do you need for 500.0 mL of 0.250 M solution?

  1. Convert volume to litres: 500.0 mL = 0.5000 L
  2. Moles needed: n = 0.250 mol/L × 0.5000 L = 0.125 mol
  3. Molar mass of NaCl = 22.990 + 35.45 = 58.44 g/mol
  4. Mass: 0.125 mol × 58.44 g/mol = 7.31 g

Making it in the lab

Weigh the solid, dissolve it in less water than you need, transfer to a volumetric flask, then add water up to the line. The volume in the formula is the final solution volume, not the volume of water you add.
