The ideal gas law

PV = nRT

  • P: pressure   V: volume   n: moles   T: temperature in kelvin
  • R = 8.314 L·kPa/(mol·K) = 0.08206 L·atm/(mol·K). Pick the R that matches your pressure unit.

Unit conversions

  • K = °C + 273.15
  • 1 atm = 101.325 kPa = 760 mmHg
  • 1 L = 1000 mL

Worked example

What volume does 2.00 mol of gas take up at 25.0 °C and 101.3 kPa?
T = 25.0 + 273.15 = 298.15 K
V = nRT ÷ P = (2.00 × 8.314 × 298.15) ÷ 101.3 = 48.9 L

Most common mistake

Using °C instead of kelvin. Gas laws only work with absolute temperature, so always convert first.
